# Workspace Home — Visual Direction

Version: `V1.0 High Fidelity`

---

## 1. Product Feeling

Workspace Home should feel like:

> 一个现代影视工作室的数字前厅。

The page combines:

- Apple: clarity and calm；
- Figma: workspace familiarity；
- Adobe Creative Cloud: creation entry；
- Runway: AI production assistance；
- Frame.io: visual project continuity。

Do not copy any single product.

---

## 2. Core Impression

Within 5 seconds the user should understand:

1. this is their creative workspace；
2. active films are ready to continue；
3. AI assistant knows the current production context；
4. creating a new film is easy。

---

## 3. Visual Composition

Use:

- cinematic project covers；
- large but restrained welcome visual；
- clean white space in Light mode；
- deep elevated surfaces in Dark mode；
- teal as a precise action/accent color；
- soft blue-gray production context；
- rounded but not playful geometry。

Avoid:

- admin dashboard grid；
- wall of metrics；
- generic gray placeholders；
- text-only project cards；
- dense tables；
- overly neon cyberpunk visuals。

---

## 4. Page Rhythm

Wide layout:

```text
Warm welcome + immediate action + AI support
↓
Current projects
↓
Production context
↓
Recent progress
```

Mobile preserves the focused order from `RESPONSIVE_SPEC.md`: welcome, immediate
action, current projects, then AI support and production context.

The page must feel active without feeling busy.

---

## 5. Imagery

Visual assets are essential.

The page requires:

- one workspace hero image；
- at least four approved project covers；
- user/team avatars；
- restrained AI assistant illustration/icon；
- one consistent local tool/action icon family。

No empty black frames.

---

## 6. Light Mode

Fully supported. The page inherits the root ACS theme; current first-use default is
Dark, not a page-level Light override.

Characteristics:

- warm neutral canvas；
- white elevated cards；
- teal primary actions；
- cinematic imagery provides contrast；
- borders remain visible but quiet；
- shadows only on floating/hero surfaces。

---

## 7. Dark Mode

Characteristics:

- deep navy-black canvas；
- elevated cool-gray panels；
- imagery remains bright enough to identify；
- teal glow limited to active actions and AI states；
- no text contrast loss；
- no flat black voids。

---

## 8. Brand Presence

Header must use official 镜构智能 logo asset.

Text-only brand replacement is not accepted when the asset exists.

Company name may appear in footer or account information, not as dense legal text in the header.
