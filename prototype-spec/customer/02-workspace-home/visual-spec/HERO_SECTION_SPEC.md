# Workspace Home — Hero Section Specification

Version: `V1.0 High Fidelity`

---

## 1. Hero Role

Hero is not a marketing banner.

It is the user's current creative context.

It must communicate:

- welcome；
- current workspace；
- current production；
- immediate next action。

---

## 2. Desktop Composition

Hero uses a large cinematic background.

Recommended aspect within grid:

- minimum height: 300px；
- preferred height: 320–360px；
- content aligned left；
- visual subject aligned right；
- bottom status strip overlays or sits inside the card。

Image subject:

- professional film camera overlooking a cinematic landscape；
- or a production-stage image related to the active project；
- no text baked into the image。

---

## 3. Required Copy

Eyebrow:

`CREATOR WORKSPACE`

Greeting:

`欢迎回来，张导 👋`

Description:

`镜构智能创作云，让每一次创作都更有想象力`

Product label:

`镜构智能创作云`

Badge:

`专业版`

Primary CTA:

`继续制作`

Secondary CTA:

`查看项目`

---

## 4. Context Metrics

The hero may display a compact bottom strip:

```text
今日创作时长     制作处理中     存储空间          团队成员
3.6 小时         2 项制作中     68.4 / 500 GB     8 人 · 在线 3 人
```

Rules:

- use one horizontal strip on desktop；
- 2×2 on mobile；
- supporting context only；
- no trend arrows unless backed by real data；
- no infrastructure terms beyond customer-readable labels。

---

## 5. Hero Visual Treatment

Allowed:

- image gradient mask；
- soft mist/light overlay；
- subtle glass context strip；
- brand teal accent line；
- low-contrast decorative geometry。

Forbidden:

- abstract empty frames；
- dashboard widgets as the hero visual；
- charts as hero imagery；
- excessive glow；
- dark overlay that hides the image。

---

## 6. Dark Theme

Use dark-specific or theme-safe image treatment.

Text must remain readable without turning the entire image black.

---

## 7. Mobile

- image becomes background or top media；
- text remains above image gradient；
- CTAs full-width or two equal buttons；
- metrics 2×2；
- minimum readable hero height；
- no cropped face/subject at focal point。
